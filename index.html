About Me

A bit about myself

I'm a first-year student in the BSc (Hons) Ethical Hacking and Network Security programme at NIBM, delivered in collaboration with Coventry University. I'm currently in the Diploma in Network Engineering stage, building a foundation in computer science, programming, and networking before moving into more advanced security modules.

I love making games, Windows apps, Android apps — anything tech. I'm not just a "cybersecurity student" or a "vibe coder." I'm someone who is genuinely passionate about the entire tech field — AI, computing, and everything in between. The problem? It's so big, I don't know where it starts or where it ends. But that's what makes it exciting.

I chose cybersecurity because I'm from Sri Lanka. We're not the most technologically advanced country, but we have real problems — scams, cyber crimes, and people getting exploited every day. I want to help fix that. I love my country, and I want to be someone who protects it.

This isn't about getting a job or chasing money. I genuinely love technology. I want to learn everything — every language, every system, every concept — before I leave this world. I want to be a responsible person who contributed something meaningful. I'll do it. I know I will.

I speak Sinhala and English, and I'm looking forward to connecting with others in networking, security, and software development as I grow in this field.
